## Availability
Cash on delivery (COD) is available on orders up to INR 5,000 to serviceable pincodes. Orders above INR 5,000 must be paid online. COD is not available for subscriptions, gift cards, or deliveries to remote areas.

## COD fee
A fee of INR 40 is added to every COD order. This fee is not refunded if you later return the items, unless the return is because of a defective, damaged or wrong item.

## How to pay
Pay the delivery agent in cash or by scanning a UPI QR code at the door. Please keep the exact amount ready, as agents do not always carry change.

## Refused or missed COD parcels
If you refuse a COD parcel or are unreachable on two delivery attempts, the parcel returns to us and the order is marked cancelled. After two refused COD orders, COD is switched off for your phone number and you will need to pay online.

## Refunds on COD orders
Because there is no online payment to reverse, we refund COD returns by bank transfer or UPI to an account you give us, or as store credit if you prefer. Store credit is added instantly once the return is approved. See the refunds article for timelines.
