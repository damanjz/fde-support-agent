## Why descale
Minerals in water leave white scale inside kettles and brewers. Scale slows heating, makes brewing uneven, and can damage heating elements. Scale damage is not covered by warranty, so regular descaling matters.

## How often
Descale every 1 to 3 months. Do it monthly if you live in a hard-water area, or if you see white flakes or notice longer heating times.

## Kettle method
Fill the kettle with water and add 10 g of citric acid per litre, which is about two teaspoons. Boil, switch off, and let it sit for 20 minutes. Empty it and rinse it three times with clean water, then boil a full kettle of plain water once and discard it.

## Electric brewer method
Mix 10 g of citric acid per litre in the water tank. Run a full brew cycle without coffee or a filter, discard the water, then run two cycles of clean water.

## Do not
- Do not use vinegar in the kettle, as the smell lingers and it can affect seals.
- Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive pads.
- Do not descale the grinder. It is a dry-only appliance.

Citric acid is available in the store.
