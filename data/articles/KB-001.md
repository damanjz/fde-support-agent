## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 6 business days from the date your order is dispatched. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available in metro cities only (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune). Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays.

## Dispatch
Orders are dispatched within 1 business day of being placed. Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST on Saturday, or at any time on a Sunday or holiday, are dispatched on the next business day. Beans are roasted to order, so bean orders may take one extra day to dispatch.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free on orders of INR 999 and above. Orders below INR 999 pay a flat INR 79 for standard shipping. Express shipping is a flat INR 149 on every order, regardless of order value. Subscription orders always ship free.

## Carriers
We ship with BlueDart, Delhivery and DTDC. The carrier is chosen automatically based on your pincode, and you cannot choose it yourself. You will see the carrier name and tracking number in your shipping confirmation email. See the tracking article for how to follow your parcel.
