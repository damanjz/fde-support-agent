## What counts
An item is damaged if it arrived broken, dented or with a torn box. It is defective if it does not work as it should out of the box. It is wrong if you received a different product, size or roast from what you ordered.

## Report it quickly
Please report the problem within 48 hours of delivery so we can raise a claim with the carrier. Share your order id, clear photos of the item and the outer box, and a short unboxing video if you have one. Reports made later, but inside the 30-day return window, are still accepted, though we may need extra photos.

## What we offer
You can choose a free replacement (subject to stock) or a full refund including the shipping charge you paid. Pickup is free in all of these cases, and the INR 99 pickup fee does not apply.

## Items past 30 days
If an electrical product such as a grinder or kettle becomes faulty after 30 days, it is a warranty matter, not a return. See the warranty articles.

## Opened coffee beans
If an opened bag of beans is defective or damaged, it can still be returned. See the article on returning coffee beans.
