## What happened
Sometimes your bank takes the money, but the confirmation does not reach us because of a network timeout. In that case no order is created and you will not receive an order confirmation email or SMS.

## What to do
Wait 30 minutes and check your email and My Orders. Sometimes the order appears late. If you still have no order, do not pay a second time for the same items, unless you are happy to risk being charged twice.

## Automatic reversal
The deducted amount is automatically reversed to your original payment method by your bank within 5 to 7 business days. Larkfield does not hold the money, and we cannot speed up the bank's reversal. If the amount has not returned after 7 business days, contact your bank first with the transaction id.

## When to contact us
Write to support with the date and time, the amount, the payment method and the transaction id or screenshot from your bank. We will confirm whether we received the payment and, if we did and no order exists, we will either create the order or refund you.

## Double charges
If you were charged twice and both payments created orders, we can cancel one before it is dispatched and refund it in 5 to 7 business days.
