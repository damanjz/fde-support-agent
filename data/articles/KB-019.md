## Online payment options
You can pay with UPI (any UPI app), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), netbanking from major Indian banks, and wallets such as Paytm and PhonePe. Cards must be issued in India. International cards are not accepted at this time.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available on orders up to INR 5,000, with a INR 40 fee. See the cash on delivery article for the full rules.

## EMI
EMI is available on selected credit cards for orders of INR 3,000 and above. No-cost EMI is available on eligible orders of INR 5,000 and above. See the EMI article.

## Gift cards and store credit
Larkfield gift cards and store credit can be applied at checkout, and combined with any other payment method for the balance.

## Payment security
We do not store your card number. Payments are processed by our payment partner and are protected by your bank's OTP authentication. Larkfield staff will never ask for a card number, CVV, UPI PIN or OTP over chat, phone or email. If someone asks you for these, do not share them and tell us.

All prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and include applicable GST.
