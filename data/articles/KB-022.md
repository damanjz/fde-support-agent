## Your invoice
A GST invoice is emailed to you when your order is dispatched, and can also be downloaded from My Orders once the order is shipped. All prices on our website include GST.

## Adding a GST number
If you want to claim input tax credit for your business, add your GSTIN at checkout, before you pay. The invoice will then be issued in your business name with your GSTIN.

## Changing an invoice
We can correct the name or GSTIN on an invoice only before the order is dispatched. After dispatch, the invoice is final under GST rules, and we cannot reissue it with a GSTIN. If you made an error in the name spelling or address, contact support and we can issue a corrected invoice.

## Refunds and credit notes
When you return an item, we issue a credit note for the refunded amount. It is emailed to you after the refund is initiated.

## Invoice not received
Check your spam folder first. You can always download it from My Orders when the order is shipped or delivered. If it is not available there, write to us with your order id and we will resend it.
