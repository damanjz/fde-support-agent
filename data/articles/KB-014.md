## Warranty periods
Every Larkfield appliance and accessory carries a manufacturer warranty that starts on the delivered date.
- Grinders: 24 months
- Brewers: 12 months
- Kettles: 12 months
- Accessories: 6 months
- Coffee beans: no warranty, as they are consumable (see the bean return article for defective bags)

The exact warranty for each product is shown on its product page under Specifications.

## What is covered
The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship that appear under normal home use. This includes motor failures, heating element failures, faulty switches, and cracked parts that are not the result of a drop.

## How to prove coverage
Your order id is your proof of purchase. You do not need to keep the paper invoice, since we can find it from the order. If you bought as a gift, the order id from the gift giver also works.

## Warranty is not a return
Warranty is a repair or replacement service. It does not give you a refund. If you are within 30 days of delivery and want your money back, use the return policy instead.
