## What you can change
From My Subscriptions you can change the coffee, the bag size, the grind and how often it is delivered (every 2 weeks or every 4 weeks). You can also change the delivery address and the payment method.

## When changes apply
Changes made at least 48 hours before the next dispatch apply to that delivery. Changes made later apply from the following delivery.

## Pricing
Your subscription price follows the current listed price of the bean you pick, less 10%. If you switch to a coffee that costs more, the new price applies from the first delivery after the change. We always show you the new price before you confirm.

## Out of stock beans
If a bean is out of stock when your delivery is due, we email you and offer a substitute of similar roast level. If you do not respond, we skip that delivery without charge and move your schedule forward.

## Grind selection
Pick the grind for your brewer: whole bean, espresso, moka pot, pour-over, French press or cold brew. If you change brewer or buy a new grinder, update your grind before the next cut-off, because ground beans cannot be re-ground once roasted and packed.
