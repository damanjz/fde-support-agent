## Why grind size matters
Water pulls flavour out of coffee at a rate that depends on how fine the grind is. Too fine and the coffee tastes bitter and harsh. Too coarse and it tastes sour and thin.

## Starting points
- Espresso: fine, like table salt
- Moka pot: fine to medium-fine
- Pour-over and drip: medium-fine, like sand
- AeroPress: medium to medium-fine
- French press: coarse, like sea salt
- Cold brew: extra coarse

## Adjusting
Change only one step at a time on your grinder dial, then taste. If the coffee tastes bitter or dry, go coarser. If it tastes sour or weak, go finer. Each brewer, grinder and batch of beans is a little different, so expect to adjust when you open a new bag.

## Pre-ground coffee
When ordering ground beans, pick the grind for your brewer from the menu. Ground coffee cannot be changed after it is packed, so choose carefully. If you are unsure, pour-over grind suits most home drip brewers.

## Burr alignment
If the grind seems uneven even after cleaning, see the grinder jamming article, or make a warranty claim if the grinder is within its warranty period.
