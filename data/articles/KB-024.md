## What you get
The Larkfield bean subscription delivers freshly roasted beans on a schedule you choose, either every 2 weeks or every 4 weeks. You pick the coffee, the bag size, and whether you want it whole bean or ground for your brewer. Subscribers get 10% off the listed bean price and free shipping on every delivery.

## Roasting and delivery
Each bag is roasted to order and dispatched within 2 business days of the billing date. Standard delivery times apply after dispatch. The roast date is printed on the bag.

## Billing
Your payment method is charged 48 hours before your scheduled dispatch date. This is also the cut-off for changes: anything you change, skip or cancel before the cut-off applies to that delivery. After the cut-off, the change applies from the next delivery.

## Payment methods
Subscriptions can be paid by card or UPI autopay. Cash on delivery, EMI, net banking and wallets cannot be used for subscriptions.

## Managing it
You can skip, pause, cancel or change your subscription at any time from My Subscriptions. See the skip, pause and cancel article.
