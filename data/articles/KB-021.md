## Eligibility
EMI is available on orders of INR 3,000 and above when you pay with an eligible credit card. No-cost EMI is available on orders of INR 5,000 and above, for 3 or 6 months, on selected credit cards. The list of eligible banks is shown at checkout, and the EMI options appear only if your card qualifies.

## How no-cost EMI works
The interest your bank charges is offset by an instant discount on your order, so you pay no more in total than the listed price. The discount is shown as a line at checkout. Your bank still shows the full order amount as an EMI plan on your statement.

## Standard EMI
Standard EMI plans of 3, 6 or 9 months carry the bank's interest rate and processing fee. The exact figures are shown at checkout before you pay.

## Returns on EMI orders
If you return an item bought on EMI, we refund to your card as usual, in 5 to 7 business days. Your bank handles closing or adjusting the EMI plan, which can take one or two billing cycles. Any interest already charged by the bank on a standard EMI is decided by the bank. Larkfield cannot change it.

## Not available
EMI is not available on debit cards, UPI, wallets or cash on delivery orders, and cannot be used on subscription orders.
