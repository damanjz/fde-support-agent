## When you get your money
Refunds are initiated after we receive the returned item and finish inspecting it, which takes up to 2 business days. The money then reaches you within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider. Cancelled prepaid orders are refunded in the same 5 to 7 business days.

## Where the refund goes
Refunds are always sent back to the original payment method: card, UPI, netbanking or wallet. We cannot send a refund to a different card or account. For cash on delivery orders, we refund by bank transfer or UPI to details you provide, or as store credit.

## Store credit
Store credit is an alternative to a refund to your original payment method. It is added to your account immediately after the return is approved, is valid for 12 months, and can be used on any order.

## What is refunded
You get the price of the returned items back. Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error: a defective, damaged or wrong item. The INR 99 reverse pickup fee, when it applies, and the INR 40 COD fee are deducted or not refunded otherwise.

## Not received your refund
If it has been more than 7 business days since we told you the refund was initiated, send us the order id and the refund reference from your bank statement and we will chase it.
