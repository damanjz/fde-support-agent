## What you can change
In My Account you can update your name, email, mobile number and saved addresses. You can keep several addresses and choose a default for checkout.

## Changing email or mobile
When you change your email or mobile number, we send a one-time code to the new address or number to verify it. Until it is verified, the old details stay active. If you no longer have access to your old email, contact support and we will verify your identity using a recent order id.

## Orders already placed
Changing your profile does not change orders already placed. The address, mobile number and email on an existing order stay as they were. To change the address on an order that has not been dispatched, contact support as described in the changing your address article.

## Marketing preferences
You can turn email and SMS marketing on or off under Communication preferences. Order and shipping messages cannot be turned off while you have an active order.

## Privacy
We only share your name, address and mobile number with the carrier delivering your order. Support staff see only the last four digits of a phone number and a masked email in most views, and our assistant shows masked details too.
