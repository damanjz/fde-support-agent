## How to request deletion
Email support from your registered email address with the subject Delete my account. For your protection, we cannot process a deletion request from a different email or from chat alone. We will confirm the request and complete it within 7 days.

## Before deleting
Make sure you have no orders in progress. Orders that are still placed or shipped must be completed or cancelled first. Active subscriptions are cancelled, and any store credit or gift card balance is lost on deletion, so use it first.

## What is deleted
Your profile, saved addresses, saved preferences and marketing subscriptions are removed. You will no longer be able to sign in.

## What we keep
We must keep invoices and payment records for the period required by Indian tax law, which is 8 years. These are kept in an archived form, are not used for marketing, and are limited to the details on the invoice.

## After deleting
Deletion cannot be undone. You are welcome to open a new account at any time. Returns and warranty claims on old orders can still be made using the order id, if they are within the return or warranty period, but you will need to contact support instead of using My Orders.
