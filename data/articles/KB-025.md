## The cut-off
Subscription changes must be made at least 48 hours before your scheduled dispatch, because your payment method is charged at that point. Changes made after the cut-off take effect from the following delivery.

## Skip
You can skip a single delivery from My Subscriptions. The next delivery date moves forward by one cycle, and nothing is charged for the skipped one.

## Pause
You can pause a subscription for up to 3 months. After that it resumes automatically, and we email you a week before the restart. You can resume earlier at any time.

## Cancel
You can cancel at any time. There is no minimum commitment and no cancellation fee. If you cancel after the cut-off, the already-charged delivery still ships and you are not charged again.

## Already charged or shipped
If a delivery has been charged but not yet dispatched and you want it stopped, contact support quickly. If it has been dispatched, it follows the normal return policy: sealed bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery, and opened bags only if defective or damaged.

If you cannot log in to My Subscriptions, support can make these changes for you after verifying your registered email.
