## Creating an account
You can create a Larkfield account with your email and a mobile number, or check out as a guest. An account lets you see My Orders, track shipments, manage subscriptions and save addresses. Guest orders can still be looked up by support with your order id.

## Forgotten password
Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your registered email. We email you a reset link that works for 30 minutes. If you do not see it within a few minutes, check your spam folder and make sure you used the same email you signed up with.

## Passwords
Use at least 10 characters. For your safety, Larkfield staff and our assistant will never ask for your password. We cannot see it, and we cannot reset it for you over chat.

## Locked out
After 5 wrong attempts, sign-in is blocked for 30 minutes. You can wait or use the reset link.

## Signing in with the wrong email
If you cannot find your account, you may have used a different email or checked out as a guest. Support can look up orders with your order id, but cannot merge accounts for you. Contact support if you believe your account has been accessed by someone else, and we will lock it while we investigate.
