## The return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days count from the delivered date shown on your order, not from the date you placed the order. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories and the invoice.

## Coffee beans
Sealed, unopened bags of beans can be returned within the 30-day window. Opened bags of beans can only be returned if they are defective or damaged, for example stale, the wrong roast, or containing foreign matter. See the article on returning coffee beans.

## Grinders, brewers and kettles
Within 30 days of delivery you can return a grinder, brewer or kettle for a refund if it is unused and complete. After 30 days, returns are no longer possible. If the product is faulty, it is covered by warranty instead, and the claim goes through the warranty process (24 months for grinders, 12 months for brewers and kettles).

## Partial returns and pickup fees
You can return some items from an order and keep the rest. Pickup is free when the item is defective, damaged or the wrong item. For any other reason, a reverse pickup fee of INR 99 is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds
Refunds go to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the item. See the refunds article for details.
