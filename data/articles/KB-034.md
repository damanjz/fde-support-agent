## Water temperature
For pour-over, drip and AeroPress, use water between 92 and 96 degrees Celsius, which is about 30 seconds off the boil. For French press, use 93 to 96 degrees. For lighter roasts, go toward the hotter end, and for dark roasts toward the cooler end. Larkfield variable temperature kettles let you set these exact temperatures.

## Ratios
A good starting ratio is 1 part coffee to 16 parts water by weight. For a 240 ml cup that is 15 g of coffee. For a stronger cup use 1 to 15, and for a milder cup 1 to 17. For espresso, use about 1 part coffee to 2 parts liquid.

## Brew times
- Pour-over: 2.5 to 3.5 minutes
- French press: 4 minutes
- AeroPress: 1.5 to 2 minutes
- Moka pot: 4 to 5 minutes on a low flame
- Cold brew: 12 to 16 hours in the fridge

## Water quality
Use filtered water. Very hard water leaves scale in your kettle and affects taste, and water that is very soft can make coffee taste flat. See the descaling article for hard water care.

## Weighing
A kitchen scale makes your coffee much more consistent than using spoons. Larkfield sells a scale with a built-in timer.
