## The five statuses
Every order shows one of these statuses in My Orders and when you ask support.
- Placed: we have your order and payment, and it is being prepared. It has not been handed to the carrier yet.
- Shipped: the carrier has picked up the parcel. A carrier name and tracking number are available.
- Delivered: the carrier has marked the parcel as handed over. The delivered date starts your 30-day return window and your warranty period.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled, by you before dispatch or by us, or because a cash on delivery parcel was refused. Any online payment is refunded in 5 to 7 business days.
- Returned: the items were returned and the return has been received by us. The refund follows the refund timeline.

## How long each stage takes
An order stays placed for up to 1 business day. After that it should be shipped. If your order has been placed for more than 2 business days, contact support. Shipped orders usually become delivered in 3 to 6 business days.

## Why dates matter
The delivered date, not the order date, is used for the return window and warranty. If the delivered date on your order looks wrong, for example you received the parcel later than the date shown, contact us so we can check with the carrier.

## Looking up your order
Give support your order id and we can tell you the status, carrier, tracking number and items.
