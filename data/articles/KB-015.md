## Before you claim
Check that the product is within its warranty period, counted from the delivered date. Grinders are covered for 24 months, brewers and kettles for 12 months, and accessories for 6 months. Try the care and troubleshooting articles first, because many issues, such as a grinder jam or slow brewing, are fixed with a clean or descale.

## What to send
Contact support with your order id, a clear description of the fault, and a photo or short video showing the problem. If the product is making a noise or not powering on, a video helps us diagnose faster.

## Diagnosis and pickup
We review the claim within 2 business days. If it looks like a covered defect, we arrange a free pickup. Do not send the product to us on your own, as we cannot track parcels that were not booked through us.

## Outcome
After the product reaches our service centre, we either repair it or replace it. See the repair and replacement article for timelines. If the fault is not covered by warranty, we explain why and quote a paid repair, which you are free to decline.
