## Before dispatch
You can change the delivery address only while the order status is placed, meaning it has not been dispatched yet. Contact support with your order id and the new full address and pincode. We will confirm the change in writing. Because orders are dispatched within 1 business day, please write to us as soon as you notice a mistake.

## After dispatch
Once the status is shipped, we cannot change the address, because the parcel has already been assigned to a carrier route. You can call the carrier directly with your tracking number and ask them to hold the parcel at their local office or to redirect it, but this is at the carrier's discretion.

## If the address is wrong and the parcel is returned
If a parcel comes back to us because of an incomplete or incorrect address, we will contact you. We can re-ship to the corrected address after you pay the standard shipping charge again, or we can cancel and refund the order minus the original shipping charge.

## Updating your saved address
Changing the address in your account profile does not change the address on orders already placed. It only applies to future orders.
