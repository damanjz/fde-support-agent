## Not covered
The warranty does not cover damage that was not caused by a manufacturing defect:
- Drops, impact or crushed parts
- Liquid or water getting into the motor or electronics of a grinder or kettle base
- Use with voltage other than 230V or with a non-Larkfield power adapter
- Repairs or modifications attempted by anyone other than our service centre
- Scale build-up from not descaling a kettle or brewer
- Commercial use, such as a cafe, because our products are rated for home use

## Wear and tear parts
Consumable parts wear out and are not covered after the first 30 days. This includes grinder burrs, rubber gaskets, filters, paper filter holders and kettle seals. Burrs are expected to dull over time with regular use. Replacement burrs are sold in the store.

## Glass and ceramics
Glass carafes and ceramic parts are covered only if they arrive cracked or defective. Breakage during use is not a warranty issue. Report arrival damage within 48 hours, as described in the damaged item article.

## Cosmetic marks
Scratches, scuffs and fading that do not affect how the product works are not covered.
