## Diagnosis
Once you submit a claim, we review it within 2 business days. If it is accepted, a free pickup is booked, usually within 2 business days after that.

## Repair
Repairs are completed within 7 to 10 business days of the product reaching our service centre. We then ship the repaired product back to you free of charge by express or standard shipping, whichever is faster for your pincode. You will get a tracking number as with any shipment.

## Replacement
If the product cannot be repaired, or if a repair fails a second time, we replace it with the same model or, if that model is discontinued, the closest equivalent. Replacement shipping is free. Replacement units are dispatched within 3 business days of the decision, subject to stock.

## Warranty on the replacement
A replacement or repaired product carries the remainder of the original warranty period, with a minimum of 3 months from the date you receive it.

## While you wait
We cannot lend out loan products. If you urgently need a grinder or brewer, the store sells accessories such as hand grinders that you can use meanwhile, but this is a new purchase and the usual return policy applies to it.
