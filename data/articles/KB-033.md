## Brewer is dripping slowly
The usual causes are a grind that is too fine, a clogged filter, or scale build-up. Try a coarser grind first, and make sure paper filters are rinsed so they sit flat. If the brewer is electric and heating is slow, descale it. See the descaling article.

## Coffee tastes bitter
Bitterness usually means over-extraction. Use a coarser grind, a shorter brew time, or slightly cooler water. A dirty grinder can also give a bitter, stale taste, so clean it.

## Coffee tastes sour or weak
Sour or thin coffee usually means under-extraction. Use a finer grind, a longer brew, or hotter water. Check that you are using enough coffee. A good starting ratio is 1 part coffee to 16 parts water by weight, which is about 15 g of coffee for 240 ml of water.

## Brewer leaks or overflows
Check that the filter holder is seated correctly, and that the filter has not folded over. Check the rubber seal for debris. A cracked carafe or a seal that has split should be reported. If it arrived like that, see the damaged item article. If it happens later, see the warranty exclusions for seals and glass.
