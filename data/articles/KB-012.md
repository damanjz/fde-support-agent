## Before dispatch
You can cancel an order at no charge while its status is placed, meaning it has not been dispatched. Contact support with your order id. Because orders are dispatched within 1 business day, cancel as early as possible. Prepaid orders are refunded to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## After dispatch
Once the status is shipped, the order cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery at the door, which sends the parcel back to us, or accept it and return it within 30 days of delivery under the return policy.

## Cancelling part of an order
We can remove items from an order only before dispatch. Contact support and tell us which items to remove. The order total is adjusted and the difference refunded. A shipping charge is added if the remaining items fall below INR 999.

## Orders cancelled by us
Occasionally we cancel an order if an item runs out of stock after you paid, or if the payment could not be verified. You are refunded in full automatically, and we email you when this happens.

## Subscription orders
To stop a subscription shipment, use the skip or cancel options. See the subscription articles.
