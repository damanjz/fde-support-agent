## When you get a tracking number
As soon as your order is handed to the carrier, its status changes to shipped. At that point we email you and send an SMS with the carrier name (BlueDart, Delhivery or DTDC) and a tracking number. Before that, the order shows as placed and there is nothing to track yet.

## How to track
Open My Orders in your account and select the order to see the status and tracking number. You can also paste the tracking number on the carrier's own website. Our support team can look up an order for you if you share the order id, which looks like LC-1042.

## Tracking updates are delayed
Carrier scans can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch. A parcel that shows no movement for a day is usually still on its way between hubs. Only if there has been no update for 5 business days should you write to us, and we will raise a trace with the carrier. The delays article has the full steps.

## What the statuses mean
The order status meanings article explains placed, shipped, delivered, cancelled and returned. A status of delivered means the carrier has marked the parcel as handed over. If you did not receive it, contact us within 48 hours of that scan so we can open an investigation.
