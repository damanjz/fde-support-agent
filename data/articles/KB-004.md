## Serviceable locations
Larkfield Coffee Co. delivers to more than 19,000 pincodes across India. Enter your pincode on any product page to see whether delivery is available and the estimated date. If a pincode is not serviceable, checkout will not let you place the order.

## Remote areas
Parts of the North East, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman and Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep are served with longer timelines. Expect 6 to 9 business days for standard delivery. Express delivery and cash on delivery are not available to these areas.

## International orders
We do not ship outside India at this time. We also cannot ship to a foreign address, even if you pay with an Indian card. If you are buying a gift for someone abroad, you can send it to an address in India and arrange onward shipping yourself.

## PO boxes and offices
We cannot deliver to PO boxes. Office and co-working addresses are fine if someone can receive the parcel during working hours. Include a landmark and a working mobile number, because the carrier will call before attempting delivery.
