## Freshness
Larkfield beans are roasted to order and shipped within 3 days of roasting. The roast date is printed on the bag. Beans taste best from 5 days up to 4 weeks after the roast date, after which they slowly go flat.

## Where to keep them
Keep beans in an airtight container or in their original resealable bag with the air pressed out, in a cool and dark cupboard away from the stove, kettle and sunlight. Avoid clear jars on the counter.

## Fridge and freezer
We do not recommend keeping beans in the fridge, because moisture and food smells get into them. If you buy in bulk, you can freeze small portions in airtight bags and take them out one at a time. Let a portion come to room temperature before opening it, and do not refreeze.

## Whole beans versus ground
Whole beans keep their flavour much longer than ground coffee. If possible, grind just before you brew. Pre-ground coffee should be used within 2 weeks of the roast date.

## Stale beans
If beans arrive tasting stale compared with the roast date, that is a defect, and the bag can be returned even if opened. See the article on returning coffee beans.
