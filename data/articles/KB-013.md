## No direct exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. If you want a different colour, size, model or roast, the quickest route is to return the item under the return policy and place a new order. Doing it this way means the new item is not held up waiting for the old one to come back.

## Steps
Start a return for the original item, within 30 days of delivery. While you wait for the pickup, place a fresh order for the item you want. If the item you want is out of stock, check the stock level through support or on the product page before returning, so you are not left without either.

## Defective items
If the item you received is defective or damaged, we can send a free replacement instead of a refund. Choose replacement when you report the problem. See the damaged or defective item article.

## Within warranty
For items past the 30-day return window, a faulty product is handled under warranty. After inspection, we repair or replace it. See the warranty claim article.

## Sale and promo items
Items bought on sale or with a promo code can be returned on the same terms. The refund is the price you actually paid, after the discount.
