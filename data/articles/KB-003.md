## When is a parcel late
A parcel is late when it has not arrived within the delivery window shown at checkout. Standard delivery is 3 to 6 business days and express is 1 to 2 business days. Remote locations can take 6 to 9 business days. Festival seasons, heavy rain and strikes can add 1 to 3 days.

## What to do
First check the tracking status. If there has been no movement for 5 business days, or it has been more than 10 business days since dispatch, contact support with your order id. We will ask the carrier for a trace, which usually takes 2 to 3 business days.

## Lost parcels
If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, or the trace is still open after 7 business days, you can choose between a free re-shipment (subject to stock) or a full refund to your original payment method. Refunds follow the usual 5 to 7 business day timeline.

## Marked delivered but not received
Check with neighbours, building security and anyone else at your address first. If it is still missing, write to us within 48 hours of the delivered scan. We will request proof of delivery from the carrier and, if it cannot be produced, treat the parcel as lost and offer the re-shipment or refund above.
