## Promo codes
Larkfield promo codes are published only on our website, our official emails, and our official social media pages. One promo code can be used per order, and promo codes cannot be combined with each other. A code applies to the item price and not to shipping charges. Check the code's terms, because some exclude subscriptions, grinders or already-discounted products.

## No custom codes in chat
Our support team and our assistant cannot create custom discount codes or give a bigger discount than a published offer. If someone offers you a code that does not appear on our official channels, it is not valid.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available in INR 500, 1,000, 2,500 and 5,000. They are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Gift cards are non-refundable, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be used to buy another gift card.

## Applying codes and cards
Enter the promo code or gift card number at checkout. A gift card can be combined with a promo code and any payment method for the remainder.

## Returns with a promo code
If you return an item bought with a promo code, the refund is the amount you actually paid for it, after the discount. If the return makes the order drop below the minimum for the code, the discount value on the remaining items is not changed.
