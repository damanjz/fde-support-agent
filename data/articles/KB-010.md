## Sealed bags
A sealed, unopened bag of beans can be returned within 30 days of delivery, like any other product. Please check the roast date on the bag before ordering; if you buy several bags, only sealed ones are accepted back.

## Opened bags: the rule
Once a bag has been opened, we cannot return it to stock for hygiene and freshness reasons. Opened bags are returnable only if they are defective or damaged. That means the beans are stale or off-tasting compared with the roast date, the wrong roast or grind was sent, the bag arrived torn, or there is foreign matter in the bag. Taste preference alone, such as not liking the roast, does not qualify.

## How to report a problem
Contact support with your order id, a photo of the bag and its roast-date label, and a short description of the problem. For foreign matter or damage, a photo of the issue helps. Approved cases get a free pickup or, for low-value bags, we may refund without collecting the bag.

## Roast not to your taste
If you do not like a coffee, we cannot take it back once opened, but we are happy to suggest a different roast or grind. Our support team can recommend a style based on how you brew.

## Subscription bags
Beans received through a subscription follow the same rule.
