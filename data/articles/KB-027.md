## How you are charged
We charge your card or UPI autopay 48 hours before each dispatch. The charge appears with the description Larkfield Subscription. You will receive an email when the charge succeeds, with the next delivery date.

## When a payment fails
If the charge fails, for example because of insufficient balance or an expired card, we retry once the next day and again 3 days later. We email you each time. If both retries fail, the subscription is paused automatically, and no delivery is made.

## Fixing it
Open My Subscriptions, update your payment method, and choose Resume. Your next delivery is scheduled according to the earliest available date, usually within 2 business days of a successful payment.

## Autopay limits
Card and UPI autopay mandates follow RBI rules. Each charge above INR 15,000 needs a separate approval, which does not affect normal bean subscriptions. Your bank may send a pre-debit notification a day before each charge.

## Wrong charge
If you were charged for a delivery you had skipped or cancelled before the cut-off, write to support with your subscription details. After we verify, we will refund it within 5 to 7 business days.
