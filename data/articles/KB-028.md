## Why clean it
Coffee oils and fine powder build up inside a grinder. Over time this makes the coffee taste stale or bitter, and it can cause jams. A regular clean keeps the grinder accurate and extends the life of the burrs.

## Every 2 weeks
Unplug the grinder. Empty the hopper, remove the catch cup and tap out any loose grounds. Brush the burr chamber and exit chute with the brush that comes in the box. Wipe the outside with a dry or barely damp cloth.

## Every 2 to 3 months
Remove the upper burr following the manual and brush both burrs and the chamber thoroughly. Larkfield Grinder Cleaning Tablets can be run through the grinder to lift oils from the burrs, followed by a handful of beans to clear residue. Wipe the hopper with a damp cloth and dry it fully before putting it back.

## Do not
- Do not wash burrs or put any grinder part in a dishwasher.
- Do not pour water into the grinder body or motor housing.
- Do not use rice as a cleaner, as it can damage the burrs.

## After cleaning
Reassemble the grinder and reset your grind setting. If the grinder still jams or sounds rough, see the jamming article. Burr wear is not covered by warranty after 30 days (see warranty exclusions).
