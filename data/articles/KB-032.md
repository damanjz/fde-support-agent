## Common causes
- Very oily dark roast beans clogging the chute
- A small stone or foreign object in the beans
- Burrs set too fine for the amount of coffee in the hopper
- Old coffee residue packed in the chamber

## What to try
Unplug the grinder. Empty the hopper, remove the catch cup and take out the upper burr as shown in the manual. Brush out the burr chamber and chute. Check for stones and husks. Reassemble, set the grind a few steps coarser, put a small amount of beans in, and run it.

## If it keeps stopping
Do not force the motor. Grinders have a thermal cut-out that stops the motor if it overheats. Let it rest for 30 minutes before trying again. Avoid running it for more than a minute at a time with a full hopper of fine settings.

## If it still does not work
A grinder that will not start, that makes a grinding or burning smell, or that stops repeatedly after cleaning may have a motor fault. Grinders have a 24-month warranty, so start a warranty claim with a short video of the problem. See the warranty claim article.

## If you are within 30 days
If the grinder is within 30 days of delivery and faulty, it can be returned or replaced instead of repaired, with free pickup.
