## The short answer
After 30 days from delivery, grinders, brewers and kettles can no longer be returned for a refund. If the product has a fault, it is covered by warranty for as long as its warranty period runs, which is 24 months for grinders and 12 months for brewers and kettles.

## Why the difference
The 30-day return window is a change-of-mind window and applies to unused products. Warranty is for products that develop a manufacturing defect with use. Using a grinder for a few weeks and then deciding you do not like it is not a defect.

## What to do
If your grinder or brewer has stopped working or is not performing, make a warranty claim with your order id and a photo or video. See the warranty claim article. We will pick it up for free, then repair or replace it.

## If there is no fault
If the product works properly and you simply want to return it after 30 days, we cannot accept the return. We are happy to suggest settings or accessories that may help.

## Within 30 days
If you are still within 30 days of delivery, the product is unused and complete, you can return it. A defective product within 30 days can be returned or replaced at no pickup cost.
