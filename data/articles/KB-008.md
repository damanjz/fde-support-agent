## Before you start
Check that your order is in delivered status and that it is within 30 days of the delivered date. Make sure the item is unused and has its original box and accessories. Opened bags of beans are only returnable if defective or damaged.

## Start the return
Message our support chat or email support with your order id, the items you want to return and the reason. You can also open My Orders and choose Return on the order. The assistant or an agent will confirm the details with you before the return request is created, so nothing is submitted without your approval.

## Pickup
After your return request is approved, we schedule a free reverse pickup within 2 business days, except where the INR 99 pickup fee applies (see the return policy). The carrier will try to collect up to 3 times. Pack the item in its original box with the invoice and hand it over.

## After pickup
We inspect the item at our warehouse within 2 business days of receiving it. If everything is in order, your refund is initiated straight away and reaches you in 5 to 7 business days. If an item fails inspection, for example signs of use or missing parts, we will contact you before rejecting the return.
