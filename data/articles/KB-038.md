## Return window
Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in original packaging. Requests made after 7 days of delivery cannot be accepted. Grinders and brewers follow the same 7-day window.

## Coffee beans
Unopened bags of coffee beans can be returned within 7 days. Opened bags cannot be returned under any circumstances.

## How to return
Email returns@larkfield.example.com with your order id. We will arrange a pickup, for which a fee of INR 149 is deducted from your refund, regardless of the reason for the return.

## Refunds
Refunds are processed within 10 business days after we receive the item, to your original payment method. Cash on delivery orders are refunded only as store credit.

## Damaged items
Damaged items must be reported within 24 hours of delivery with photos. Reports received after 24 hours may not be accepted.

Warranty claims are handled separately, and grinders and brewers carry a 12-month warranty.
